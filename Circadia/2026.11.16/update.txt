New sun: a white-hot disc with a corona, sized to vanish exactly at full night. Sun and minute colours now change once per twilight stage.
New optional settings (both off by default): "Show the moon's dark side" and "Horizon glow at twilight".
The new moon is now the same size as every other phase.
MIP watches keep the full face (sky, time and data fields) in low power.
On Connect IQ 4 watches the moon is always drawn the same way, with the dark side black or dim grey; nothing is drawn outside the moon any more.
On watches with a GPU, the moon shows its true shape for the exact phase (the dividing line moves a pixel at a time, crescents and gibbous moons are mirror images) and turns to face the sun in 5° steps. Older watches keep the original 12 phases.
AMOLED: brighter, smoother sun halo (12 rings); the horizon glow is a soft crescent like the halo, fading inward and tapering to a point at each end.
MIP: a more compact sun, always three layers (ball and two thin rings); the horizon glow is two tapering crescents in the halo's two colours, and neighbouring bands always differ clearly.
With the dark side off, the new moon is not drawn at all.
